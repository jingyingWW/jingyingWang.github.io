About

I am a PhD student in the SNSF research project Labour Markets as Emergent Mobility Networks at the Chair of Prof. Per Block, University of Zurich, since 2023, co-supervised by Prof. Christiaan Monden at the University of Oxford.

My main research interests are social mobility, social stratification in the labour market, and advanced statistical modelling. In my first doctoral project, I investigate how partner choices are structured around occupations beyond endogamy and homogamy; my second doctoral project examines patterns of cross-occupation moves that reflect how people with different educational attainment follow the career line. I am currently working on how occupational gender segregation persists despite people's frequent occupation changes.

I hold an MSc in Social Research from the University of Edinburgh and a Diploma in Japanese Language and Literature (minor in Psychology) from Tsinghua University.
