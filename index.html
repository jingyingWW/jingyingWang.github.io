About

I am a PhD student in the SNSF research project Labour Markets as Emergent Mobility Networks at the Chair of Prof. Per Block, University of Zurich, since 2023, co-supervised by Prof. Christiaan Monden at the University of Oxford. My dissertation is titled "Networks of Occupations: Marriage, Career Line, and Gender Segregation."

My main research interests are social mobility, social stratification in the labour market, and advanced statistical modelling. In my first doctoral project, I investigate how partner choices are structured around occupations beyond endogamy and homogamy; my second doctoral project examines patterns of cross-occupation moves that reflect how people with different educational attainment follow the career line. I am currently working on how part-time employment channels workers into distinct positions in the labour market.

I hold an MSc in Social Research (with Distinction) from the University of Edinburgh and a Diploma in Japanese Language and Literature (minor in Psychology) from Tsinghua University. In 2025 I was a research visitor at Nuffield College, University of Oxford.
